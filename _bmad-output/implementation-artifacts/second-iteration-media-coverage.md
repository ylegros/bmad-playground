## Verification

Every candidate portrait and logo was inspected visually using contact sheets; final runtime tests decode every mapped photo and team mark in both seasons. One ambiguous actor Mark Webber candidate was rejected and replaced with F1 driver Mark Webber at the 2017 United States GP. A 2016 Williams W candidate was rejected for the 2010 AT&T identity. Tiny logo files whose sole reuse rationale was their pixel size were not used.

Actual desktop and narrow comparison screenshots were inspected; the 200% screenshot exposed scorecard clipping missed by the page-overflow assertion. Panel-width container rules now reflow the summary and tests assert every summary child remains inside its bounds. All image-error fallbacks remain tested separately from successful image loading.

The Mercedes GP 2010 wordmark was checked against the official team launch photograph at https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/news/on-this-day-mercedes-launch-f1-team-in-2010 . Sauber uses the historical red S emblem, not BMW 2005–2009 or later Stake branding. Lotus Racing uses the 2010 green Lotus F1 Racing wordmark, not Enstone Lotus. Red Bull 2010 source explicitly identifies 2005–2012 use; it is not reused as the 2021 Honda identity. Ferrari uses the historical shield photograph; McLaren 2021 uses its standalone speedmark. Other original-source descriptions identify the Renault diamond, Virgin, Hispania, Alpine and Uralkali Haas marks; their source pages and raw metadata are retained in the acquisition directories.
